# DOOR//CLUB Texture Generation Prompts

Each numbered block below is a complete standalone prompt. Do not prepend a separate shared style prefix. New creative direction: DOOR//CLUB is a highly designed contemporary music venue, not an industrial bunker. The visual identity combines soft concrete, smooth microcement, ivy, lawn strips, large sculptural cacti, agave, grasses, moss, warm stone, smoked glass, and quiet architectural lighting. Use 2026 design cues: integrated biophilia, soft modernism, desert modernism, warm minimalism, sensory hospitality design, indoor-outdoor flow, layered greenery, natural textures, deep "midnight garden" greens, warm amber lighting, and carefully controlled orange/dark green club light floods. Keep the late 90s / early 2000s PS2 game texture aesthetic, but make it stylish, calm, expensive, and memorable.
